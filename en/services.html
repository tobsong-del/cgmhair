01 · Cut

CGM Curl Cut

A dry cut designed for natural curls. Instead of cutting everything wet at once, we shape the hair while watching where each curl settles. Includes shampoo and a basic CGM hair check.

Price₩68,000
TimeAbout 1 hour
IncludesShampoo · basic CGM hair check
Who it's for
Anyone who wants to bring out their curls instead of straightening them, anyone growing out Korean straightening, and anyone who wants a curly bob, shag, wolf cut, mullet or side layers
What we check
We listen to the style you want and your daily routine, and check your curl shrinkage and drying pattern.
Process
  1. Consultation and hair observation
  2. Shampoo
  3. Dry cut, curl by curl
  4. Styling
  5. Home-care advice

02 · Design & Perm

Curl Design & Perm

While you grow out Korean straightening, new natural curls and old straightened lengths sit side by side. The CGM Blend Perm is designed to join the two naturally. Whether it suits you depends on your treatment history and hair condition, which we check in consultation.

03 · Consultation

Curl Consultation

Every service at CGM HAIR starts with a consultation where we observe your curls. We keep a client record, noting separately what you tell us, what we see and how we read it, so we can compare on your next visit. We also advise people thinking about growing out straightening.

BasicBasic CGM hair check included in the CGM Cut
In-depthIn-depth advice in our classes
  • Shrinkage
  • How hair takes water
  • Response to products and treatments
  • Drying pattern
  • Visual density
  • Where and how frizz appears
  • How long curls hold
Before your visit
If you can, come without products in your hair. We need to see your curls as they are to find exactly what needs attention.

These are observation points for a salon consultation, not a medical diagnosis or a standardized test.

04 · Care & Education

Curl Care & Education

Curls are maintained at home, after you leave the salon. You learn curl-care methods such as co-wash, low-poo, plopping and the bowl method, adjusted to how your hair responds. We focus on finding a routine that fits your life rather than adding more products.
