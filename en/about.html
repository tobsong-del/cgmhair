How we work

  1. Four services designedWe treat curly hair as the starting point for every consultation and design the cut from there.
  2. Through the transition togetherWe help clients understand their own curls as they stop straightening.
  3. Care that continues at homeWith every service, we explain how to care for your hair at home.

Story

A straight-haired stylist's curl story

My own hair isn't curly. So instead of claiming I already know curls, I start by observing them every time.

How different the curls look wet and dry, how they react to water and products, where the frizz comes from. I write it down for every client, and when you come back we open the record together and compare.

That's how, in September 2025, I opened CGM HAIR, a small salon in Sanbon just for curls.

I wanted to celebrate the day people who hid their curls with straightening finally meet their own curls, so I made 23 October 'Straightening-Free Day'. The stories we collect become our curly hair newspaper, CGM HAIR NEWS.

I believe curls are not a problem to fix but a character to understand. I'd love to write your curl story with you here.

Song Sukyung, owner of CGM HAIR
