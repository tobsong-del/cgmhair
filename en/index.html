Curl Consultation

Before we cut, we observe

We look at your curls using seven criteria and keep a client record. We note what you tell us, what we observe and how we interpret it separately, then compare the changes on your next visit.

Seven points on our hair record

  • 01Shrinkage
  • 02How hair takes water
  • 03Response to products and treatments
  • 04Drying pattern
  • 05Visual density
  • 06Where and how frizz appears
  • 07How long curls hold

These are observation points for a salon consultation, not a medical diagnosis or a standardized test.

How we observe and design the cut

  1. Hearing what you wantWe talk about your concerns, the look you want and your daily routine.
  2. Observing your curlsWe look at your curls wet and dry, and check the seven points.
  3. Designing the cutWe choose the length and layers where your curls will settle, and explain the expected result first.
  4. Cutting and stylingWe cut curl by curl, watching the shape, and finish the style.
  5. Care at homeWe show you how to wash, which products to use and how to dry your curls.
  6. Comparing on your next visitWe look at past records and photos together to see how your curls have changed.
