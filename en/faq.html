FAQ

Curly hair questions, answered

Common questions about curly hair, transitioning from straightening and our services. Anything else? Send us a DM on Instagram.

Q01

Do I have to straighten my curly hair?

No. Straightening (Korean magic straight perm) is a choice. At CGM HAIR we see curls as a character to understand, not a problem to fix, and we suggest cuts and care that bring out your curls. Whether to keep straightening or stop is something we decide together in the consultation, based on your hair and lifestyle.

Q02

How do I start transitioning from straightened hair?

In the consultation we first look at how much straightened hair is left and what your new curls look like. Then we choose between a big chop (CGM Cut), which removes the straightened lengths in one go, and a CGM Blend Perm, which joins the straightened lengths to your curls so you can keep your length. You can see both in our real transition stories.

Q03

How long does a transition take?

It varies a lot from person to person: how much straightened hair is left, how fast your hair grows, and whether you cut it off at once or grow it out. A big chop lets you start with no straightened hair the same day; a blend perm lets you keep your length while the straightened part grows out. We explain the likely process after looking at your hair.

Q04

What is a dry cut for curly hair?

Instead of cutting everything wet at once, we shape the hair dry, watching where each curl settles. Curls shrink by different amounts as they dry, so cutting dry lets us set length and layers while seeing the finished shape. Our CGM Cut costs ₩68,000 and includes shampoo and a basic CGM hair check.

Q05

What is a blend perm for curly hair?

While you grow out straightening, new natural curls and old straightened lengths sit side by side. The CGM Blend Perm adds curls that match your texture to the straightened part so the two join naturally. It costs ₩230,000 including a treatment and basic CGM check; whether it suits you depends on your treatment history and hair condition.

Q06

Why does curly hair puff up in humidity?

Hair changes shape when it absorbs moisture from the air. Because curly hair bends and twists, different parts absorb moisture differently, so curls lose definition and frizz more easily. Research has also reported that sun exposure (UV) alters the hair's inner protective structure (CMC), making it more sensitive to humidity. Read more, with care tips, in our 20 November 2026 issue.

Q07

Can curly hair be cut short?

Yes. Curls shrink as they dry, so hair can look much shorter than when wet. That's why we check your shrinkage before choosing the length. See a jaw-length curly bob and short men's curly cuts in our client stories.

Q08

What happens in a CGM HAIR consultation?

Every service starts with observing your curls. We look at seven points—shrinkage, how hair takes water, response to products and treatments, drying pattern, visual density, where and how frizz appears, and how long curls hold—and record them. We note what you tell us, what we observe and how we interpret it separately, and compare on your next visit. These are salon observation criteria, not a medical diagnosis or standardized test.

Q10

Does CGM stand for Curly Girl Method?

No. The CGM in CGM HAIR stands for Curly Gorgeous Movement, our salon's brand name. It is different from the Curly Girl Method, a well-known curly hair routine. We suggest methods such as co-wash or low-poo only when they suit how your hair responds.
